Meet the Editor

Hi, I'm Krishna Dubey.

I'm a Mumbai based retoucher specializing in high-end luxury real estate and interior imagery. What started in 2021 as a way to simply pay the bills quickly turned into something much deeper.

Get in Touch

The Journey

I began working with photographers across real estate and interiors, learning the craft from the ground up. Over time, I noticed a gap — most edits were technically correct, but they lacked intention, mood, and refinement. That pushed me to go deeper. Not just into editing, but into understanding:

  • How photographers shoot
  • How light shapes perception
  • How a space should feel, not just look

What Makes My Work Different

For me, this is not a transaction — it's a collaboration. I work closely with photographers, often going beyond just editing:

  • Sharing shooting techniques
  • Suggesting exposure methods
  • Helping refine their visual direction

Over time, many of my clients have become long-term collaborators — and even friends. Because great work doesn't come from outsourcing. It comes from alignment.
